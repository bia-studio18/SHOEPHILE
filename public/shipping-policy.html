Shipping Policy

SHOEPHILE ships across Pakistan.

Shipping Fee

A flat shipping fee of PKR 300 applies to all orders.

Free Shipping

Free shipping is available on all orders with a subtotal (after any discounts) of PKR 3,000 or more.

Processing & Delivery

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. You will receive order confirmation and status updates by email.
