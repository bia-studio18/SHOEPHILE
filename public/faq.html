Frequently Asked Questions

What products does SHOEPHILE sell?

SHOEPHILE specialises exclusively in women's flats — elegant, comfortable styles designed for everyday sophistication.

What sizes are available?

We offer European sizes 36, 37, 38, 39, 40 and 41. Availability varies by style and colour; out-of-stock sizes are clearly marked on the product page.

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, select your size and colour, add to bag, then proceed to checkout. You can order as a guest — no account required. Fill in your details, choose a payment method and place the order.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Card payments, and NayaPay. Select your preferred method at checkout.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes. COD is available across Pakistan. Please keep the exact amount ready for the courier.

What are the shipping charges?

A flat shipping fee of PKR 300 applies to all orders. Shipping is free on orders above PKR 3,000.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery across Pakistan usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your city.

How can I track my order?

Use the Track Order page with your order number (e.g. SP-1001). You will also receive email updates when your order status changes.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a 10-day return and exchange window. Items must be unused, in original condition with tags. See our Return & Exchange Policy for full details.

How do I request a return or exchange?

Email us at shoephile218@gmail.com with your order number and reason. Our team will guide you through the process.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Eligible returns may be refunded according to the original payment method, or exchanged for another size/style subject to availability.

How do I contact support?

Email shoephile218@gmail.com or use the Contact form. We aim to respond within 24–48 hours.
