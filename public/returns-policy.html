Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to love your SHOEPHILE flats. If something is not right, we are here to help.

Eligibility

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 10 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in original condition, with all tags and packaging intact.

How to Request

Email shoephile218@gmail.com with your order number, the item(s) concerned, and whether you prefer a return (refund) or exchange. Our team will provide instructions.

Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or style are subject to availability. We will confirm options once we receive your request.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method where possible. Please allow a few business days after we receive the returned item.

Non-returnable

Items that show signs of wear, damage caused after delivery, or missing tags/packaging may not be accepted for return.

For any questions, contact us at shoephile218@gmail.com.
